# NetSniper v2.1 Stage 0B — Taxonomy and Uncertainty Policy

Status: Approved design baseline for Stage 0C and the v2.1 implementation.

## 1. Authoritative classification architecture

NetSniper v2.1 will have one authoritative scoring implementation and one
authoritative evidence-profile data source.

- `classification/evidence_profiles.json` remains the machine-readable scoring
  data authority after its v2.1 migration.
- Runtime classification and fixture replay must execute the same scoring
  implementation.
- `netsniper.sh` may orchestrate classification and emit compatibility fields,
  but it must not maintain a second independent scoring table.
- Legacy fields remain compatibility projections, not a second taxonomy.

## 2. Canonical classification axes

### Device family

Allowed values:

- `network_infrastructure`
- `compute_host`
- `client_endpoint`
- `printer`
- `surveillance_device`
- `storage_appliance`
- `voice_device`
- `power_device`
- `iot_embedded`
- `unknown`

A device family describes what kind of device is being observed. It must not be
inferred solely from a service role.

### Roles

Initial controlled role vocabulary:

- `gateway`
- `wireless_access_point`
- `managed_switch`
- `security_gateway`
- `domain_controller`
- `dns_server`
- `web_server`
- `web_application_host`
- `database_server`
- `container_host`
- `container_orchestrator`
- `hypervisor`
- `file_server`
- `nas_service`
- `print_service`
- `camera`
- `network_video_recorder`
- `voip_endpoint`
- `pbx`
- `ups_monitor`
- `mail_server`
- `development_admin_interface`

A host may have zero, one, or multiple roles. Role evidence does not
automatically determine device family.

### Platform

Initial controlled platform vocabulary:

- `windows`
- `linux`
- `macos`
- `ios`
- `android`
- `network_os`
- `embedded`
- `unknown`

Platform is independent from device family and roles.

## 3. Decision model

Each family candidate and role candidate receives its own decision.

Allowed decisions:

- `classified`: calibrated evidence meets the classification threshold and no
  review condition blocks classification.
- `possible`: a credible leading candidate exists, but evidence is insufficient
  for classification.
- `review`: evidence conflicts, candidates are too close, or policy requires
  operator review.
- `unknown`: no defensible candidate reaches the minimum evidence threshold.

`Ambiguous Device` is not a device family in v2.1. Ambiguity is represented by
`review`, contradictions, secondary candidates, and uncertainty reasons.

## 4. Confidence model

Confidence remains an integer from 0 through 100.

Canonical confidence bands:

- `none`: 0
- `weak`: 1–39
- `possible`: 40–69
- `strong`: 70–89
- `high`: 90–100

Confidence band and decision are separate. A high raw score may still produce
`review` when contradictions or candidate ambiguity are present.

Minimum default thresholds:

- candidate visibility: 1
- possible: 40
- classified: 70
- high-confidence classification: 90

## 5. Confidence caps and review rules

- Port-only evidence may not exceed 39.
- Vendor-only or hostname-only evidence may not exceed 39.
- A single medium-reliability source may not exceed 49.
- A single high-reliability source may not exceed 69 unless the Stage 0 corpus
  proves that the signature is uniquely identifying.
- Strong classification normally requires at least two independent evidence
  sources, including at least one medium- or high-reliability source.
- High classification normally requires either two independent high-reliability
  sources or one high-reliability source plus multiple corroborating sources.
- A strong contradiction forces `review` and caps the affected candidate below
  the classified threshold.
- Two credible candidates within the calibrated ambiguity margin force
  `review`.
- Missing evidence must be recorded explicitly. Collector failure may lower the
  observation-quality ceiling but must not be treated as negative evidence.
- Lack of an observed service in a partial scan is not evidence that the service
  is absent.
- Device family may remain `unknown` while one or more service roles are
  classified.

## 6. Required uncertainty reasons

Unknown, possible, and review outcomes must include at least one controlled
uncertainty reason.

Initial vocabulary:

- `no_classification_evidence`
- `insufficient_evidence_diversity`
- `port_only_evidence`
- `vendor_only_evidence`
- `hostname_only_evidence`
- `collector_unavailable`
- `collector_failed`
- `partial_scan`
- `strong_contradiction`
- `candidate_scores_too_close`
- `family_not_inferable_from_role`
- `identity_instability`
- `unsupported_signature`
- `legacy_projection_only`

## 7. Legacy compatibility projections

The existing fields remain available for DeltaAegis v0.44.1 and older
consumers:

- `primary_type`
- `device_type`
- `device_type_confidence`
- `confidence`
- `confidence_label`
- `decision`
- `secondary_candidates`
- `contradictions`
- `calibration_reason`

The v2.1 result is authoritative. Legacy fields are derived deterministically
from it.

Required compatibility mappings include:

- `Router / Gateway` → family `network_infrastructure`, role `gateway`
- `Wireless Access Point` → family `network_infrastructure`, role
  `wireless_access_point`
- `Active Directory / Domain Controller` → role `domain_controller`; family
  requires independent host evidence
- `Web Server / Web Application Host` → roles `web_server` and/or
  `web_application_host`; family requires independent evidence
- `Container Infrastructure` → role `container_host` or
  `container_orchestrator`; family requires independent evidence
- `IP Camera / NVR` → family `surveillance_device`, role `camera` and/or
  `network_video_recorder`
- `Network Printer / MFP` and historical printer aliases → family `printer`,
  role `print_service`
- `NAS / Storage Appliance` → family `storage_appliance`, role `nas_service`
  and/or `file_server`
- `Database Server` → role `database_server`; family requires independent
  evidence
- `Unknown` → family `unknown`, decision `unknown`
- `Ambiguous Device` and `Unknown / Ambiguous` → family `unknown`, decision
  `review`
- `Managed Switch / Network Infrastructure` → family
  `network_infrastructure`, role `managed_switch`
- `Security Appliance` → family `network_infrastructure`, role
  `security_gateway`
- `Hypervisor / Virtualization Host` → role `hypervisor`; family requires
  independent evidence
- `VoIP Phone / PBX` → family `voice_device`, role `voip_endpoint` and/or `pbx`
- `UPS / Power Device` → family `power_device`, role `ups_monitor`
- `Development / Admin Interface` → role `development_admin_interface`; family
  requires independent evidence
- `IoT / Embedded Device` → family `iot_embedded`
- `Mail Server` → role `mail_server`; family requires independent evidence
- `Windows Server`, `Windows Workstation`, and `Linux Server` project into the
  platform axis and an independently supported family.

Historical `contradiction_review` maps to canonical `review`.

## 8. Stage 0C corpus requirements

The labeled corpus must test family, role, platform, uncertainty, contradiction,
partial-scan, and legacy-projection behavior independently.

It must contain both deterministic synthetic fixtures and sanitized real-world
fixtures. Small fixture sets may serve as regression gates, but they must not be
used to make statistically unsupported accuracy claims.
